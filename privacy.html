Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. Overview

JenniferslawAction.org (the “Site”) is operated by Jennifer’s Law Action (“we” or “us”). This policy explains what information the Site collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

2. Information You Provide

You can browse the Site without providing any personal information. We collect personal information only when you choose to submit it: when you sign a petition, subscribe to updates, contact us, or make a gift. Depending on the form, this may include your name, email address, city, state, ZIP code, and any comment or message you write.

3. Petitions

Petition signatures are stored on our own web server, not on a third-party petition platform. When a petition is delivered to the people it is addressed to, the delivery includes signers’ names, cities and states, and any comment they chose to write. Email addresses are never delivered, published, or shared; they are used only to keep each person to one signature per petition and, if you checked “Keep me updated,” to send you campaign updates. If you checked “Show my name and city on the list of signers,” your name and city may appear on the Site.

4. Gifts

Gifts are processed by third-party payment services (such as Zeffy, PayPal, or your bank for Zelle), which handle your payment information under their own privacy policies. We receive your name, email address, and the amount of your gift, and we use them to acknowledge your gift and keep required records.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit to record and deliver petitions, respond to your messages, send updates you asked for, and acknowledge gifts. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone, for any purpose.

6. Analytics and Cookies

The Site may use standard analytics tools to understand aggregate traffic. Analytics providers may set cookies; you can block or delete cookies in your browser without losing access to any part of the Site.

7. Your Choices

You may unsubscribe from email updates at any time, and you may ask us to correct or delete information you submitted by contacting us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws, and we will honor valid requests consistent with those laws.

8. Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you submit. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
