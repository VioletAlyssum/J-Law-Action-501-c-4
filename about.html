About Jennifer’s Law Action

The petitions, the public campaigns, and the legislative fight to end judicial immunity.

What We Do

Jennifer’s Law Action organizes citizens to hold judges accountable. It runs the petitions to the people who decide whether judges keep their seats, the public campaigns around judicial reappointment and retention, and the legislative campaign to end judicial immunity and create independent accountability for judges.

Who Founded It

Jennifer Canali started out knowing nothing about the law. She taught herself to do everything lawyers do and has litigated eight cases, in two counties, before 25 judges, over more than 11.5 years, at or above the level of every attorney she faced. In all that time, not one judge has ruled on the merits of her claims. She has done more than 19,000 hours of legal work without being paid a dollar for any of it. Every order is published at JennifersLaw.org.

How We Are Organized

Jennifer’s Law Action is a 501(c)(4) social welfare organization being incorporated as a Texas nonprofit corporation. It is a separate organization from Jennifer’s Law Foundation, which is being organized under section 501(c)(3) for research and public education and publishes the documented record at JennifersLaw.org, and it is separate from Jennifer Canali personally. Each keeps its own bank account, and a gift to one never pays for the work of another.

Contributions to Jennifer’s Law Action are not deductible as charitable contributions for federal income tax purposes. Jennifer’s Law Action may use contributions for lobbying and for public campaigns concerning the reappointment and retention of judges.
