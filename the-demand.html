The Campaign

The Demand

End judicial immunity. Make judges accountable to an independent body of citizens. Stop making the victim pay.

What We Are Demanding

1. End judicial immunity. Judges created the doctrine of judicial immunity for themselves, and the Supreme Court has held that it protects a judge even when the judge is accused of acting “maliciously and corruptly.” No judge should be immune for deliberate constitutional violations, corruption, fraud, retaliation, conduct outside lawful authority, or conduct causing catastrophic harm.

2. Create independent accountability. Ending immunity alone is not enough, because a lawsuit against a judge is decided by that judge’s friends and peers. Accountability has to come from outside the system:

  • a standing accountability body that reviews judges’ conduct and acts on it;
  • staffed by people who are not judges, not lawyers, not friends of the judges, and not financially connected to them;
  • answering to a board of citizens, with non-lawyers and non-judges at every level up to the top;
  • with immediate, escalating consequences: penalty, probation, and removal;
  • and with severe consequences for anyone in that chain who fails to act.

3. Stop making the victim pay. No one harmed by a judge should have to hire a lawyer, file another case, pay for another appeal, or win the approval of other judges before anything happens.

4. Put everything on the record. Every hearing recorded and livestreamed, every filing public, and every ruling explained in writing, so there is a record to hold judges to.

What Happens Without It

Judge Robert W. Rohm dismissed Jennifer Canali’s case with prejudice, sanctioned her $29,478.23, revoked her fee waiver, revoked her right to appear by Zoom, and barred her from emailing the court. After eight years of litigation and more than 11,500 hours of work on this one case, no judge has ever ruled on its merits: no judge has reviewed the facts and the evidence and applied the law to them.

To get the chance to start over from square one, Jennifer must now pay about $70,000: $29,478.23 in sanctions, $30,000 for the appeal, and $10,000 for the bond on the sanctions, and spend at least another 1,000 hours on the appeal. Judge Rohm and the colleagues who caused this will not lose a single minute of their time or a single dollar of their money. Every minute and every dollar falls on the victim.

They caused the harm. The victim pays for it.

Why Change Will Not Come From the Courts

In fiscal year 2024, 1,510 misconduct complaints were filed against federal judges; three ended in a censure or reprimand, and not one judge was suspended. The Illinois Judicial Inquiry Board received 556 requests for investigation and closed 531 of them without investigating at all. The bodies that oversee judges are run by judges and lawyers, and they protect their own.

Jennifer Canali began with no legal training. She taught herself to do everything a lawyer does and, for more than 11.5 years, has litigated at or above the level of every attorney she has faced, in eight cases before 25 judges. Not one of those judges has ever ruled on the merits of her claims. None has reviewed the facts and the evidence and applied the law to them. The last of them sanctioned her $29,478.23 and left her to pay about $70,000 for the chance to start over. Every order in her cases is published at JennifersLaw.org, so anyone can check every word. When the judges cause the harm and the victim pays for it, the system is not broken. It is working exactly as it was designed to work.

Where This Goes

Judicial immunity can be ended by Congress for federal civil-rights claims and by state legislatures for state claims. In many states, including Illinois, the system for disciplining judges is written into the state constitution, so replacing it with independent accountability takes a constitutional amendment, and a constitutional amendment takes voters. Every name on this petition is a voter who has already decided.

The Petition

To the United States Congress and the legislature of every state: We, the undersigned, demand an end to judicial immunity and the creation of independent accountability for judges: a standing body of citizens who are neither judges nor lawyers, with no ties to the courts, answering to a board of citizens, with the power to penalize, suspend, and remove judges who violate the law or the rights of the people who come before them. No one should have to file another lawsuit, or win the approval of other judges, for a judge to answer for what the judge did.
