The 2027 Reappointment Vote

You Don’t Get a Vote. They Do.

Judge Robert W. Rohm, Judge David E. Schwartz, and every other DuPage County associate judge keep their seats after June 30, 2027 only if three-fifths of the circuit judges vote yes, by secret ballot. No member of the public votes. Judges No Longer on the Bench

  • Judge Dorothy French Mallen (DuPage County): Retired on July 10, 2020, less than six months after entering the January 22, 2020 order that confined Jennifer’s case to one day.
  • Judge Richard D. Russo (DuPage County): Left the bench as an associate judge on December 31, 2018; the Chief Judge’s notice of the vacancy calls it a resignation.
  • Judge Brian J. Diamond (DuPage County): Retired as an associate judge on January 14, 2022.
  • Judge Callie L. Baird (Cook County): Retired on April 15, 2024.

These judges are documented on this site, and no petition is needed because they no longer hold office. Leaving the bench does not end what the public pays: Illinois judges who retire with the required service collect a pension for life, and under Tier 1 of the Judges’ Retirement System it rises 3% every year, compounded. About These Petitions These petitions are expressions of opinion by the people who sign them, based on the public record published on this site. They are hosted on JennifersLaw.org itself, not on a third-party petition platform, so they never close or expire: each stays open as a permanent public record, and the signatures are delivered to the decision-makers before each decision. Retention and reappointment dates come from public court and election sources as of October 16, 2026. See our Terms & Conditions.
