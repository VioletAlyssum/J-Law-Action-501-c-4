Vote No on Retaining Judge Mia S. McPherson

Circuit Judge and Chief Judge-Elect of the 18th Judicial Circuit · 2028 retention election

The Petition

We, the undersigned, will vote NO on the retention of Chief Judge-Elect Mia S. McPherson on November 7, 2028 and call on every voter in DuPage County to do the same. A judge keeps the seat only if 60% of voters say yes, and a judge who treats a case and a person this way has not earned that vote.

  • Addressed toThe voters of DuPage County
  • The decisionRetention election on November 7, 2028. A judge keeps the seat only with at least 60% “yes” votes.
  • WhyJudge McPherson is the Chief Judge-Elect of the 18th Judicial Circuit, the office that oversees every DuPage County judge documented on this site, and she has been copied on Jennifer Canali’s notices of that conduct under Rule 2.15 of the Illinois Code of Judicial Conduct. The documented record for Judge McPherson is being published on this site.
  • ExpirationNone. This petition never closes. The signatures are delivered before the decision, and the petition stays open afterward as a permanent public record.
