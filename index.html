The judges caused the harm.The victim pays for it.

Judge Robert W. Rohm dismissed Jennifer Canali’s case with prejudice, sanctioned her $29,478.23, revoked her fee waiver, revoked her right to appear by Zoom, and barred her from emailing the court. After eight years of litigation and more than 11,500 hours of work on this one case, no judge has ever ruled on its merits: no judge has reviewed the facts and the evidence and applied the law to them.

To get the chance to start over from square one, Jennifer must now pay about $70,000: $29,478.23 in sanctions, $30,000 for the appeal, and $10,000 for the bond on the sanctions, and spend at least another 1,000 hours on the appeal. Judge Rohm and the colleagues who caused this will not lose a single minute of their time or a single dollar of their money. Every minute and every dollar falls on the victim.

The Case Against Reappointing Judge Rohm

74 Reasons Not to Reappoint Judge Rohm

Judge Rohm is an associate judge. No voter ever elected him. He keeps his seat after June 30, 2027 only if three-fifths of DuPage County’s circuit judges vote yes, by secret ballot. This is part of what they would be voting to keep.

August 28 & 31, 2026

Sanctioned her $3,000 because she could not appear in person for a trial that could not occur, then dismissed her case with prejudice.

 October 28, 2026

What Has to Change

End Judicial Immunity. Make Judges Accountable Without One More Lawsuit.

End judicial immunity. No judge should be immune for deliberate constitutional violations, corruption, fraud, retaliation, conduct outside lawful authority, or conduct causing catastrophic harm.

Create independent accountability. A standing body of people who are neither judges nor lawyers, with no ties to the courts, answering to a board of citizens, with the power to penalize, suspend, and remove judges, the way every employer treats every employee.

Stop making the victim pay. No one harmed by a judge should have to file another case, pay for another appeal, or win the approval of other judges before anything happens.

About Jennifer’s Law Action

Jennifer’s Law Action was founded by Jennifer Canali. She began with no legal training, taught herself to do everything a lawyer does, and for more than 11.5 years has litigated at or above the level of every attorney she has faced, in eight cases before 25 judges. In all that time, not one of those judges has ruled on the merits of her claims. Every order in her cases is published at JennifersLaw.org, so anyone can check every word.

Jennifer’s Law Action is a 501(c)(4) social welfare organization being incorporated in Texas. It runs the petitions, the public campaigns on the reappointment and retention of judges, and the legislative campaign to end judicial immunity. It is separate from Jennifer’s Law Foundation, which publishes the record and the research.
