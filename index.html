Judges answer to no one.We, the people, are going to change that.

Jennifer’s Law Action is the campaign for judicial accountability. It organizes citizens, puts the documented record in front of the people who decide whether judges keep their seats, and campaigns for laws that end judicial immunity and make judges answer to an independent body of citizens.

Every effort to reform the courts from the inside has asked judges to hold themselves accountable. They never have, and they never will. Change has to come from outside the system, from ordinary people who refuse to accept it.

The Demand

End Judicial Immunity. Make Judges Answer to the People.

End judicial immunity. No judge should be immune for deliberate constitutional violations, corruption, fraud, retaliation, conduct outside lawful authority, or conduct causing catastrophic harm.

Create independent accountability. A standing body of people who are neither judges nor lawyers, with no ties to the courts, answering to a board of citizens, with the power to penalize, suspend, and remove judges, the way every employer treats every employee.

No lawsuit required. No one harmed by a judge should have to file another case, or win the approval of other judges, before anything happens.

Who We Are

Jennifer’s Law Action is a 501(c)(4) social welfare organization being incorporated in Texas. It was founded by Jennifer Canali, a citizen with no law degree who taught herself the law and fought eight cases before 25 judges over more than 11.5 years without winning once. It is separate from Jennifer’s Law Foundation, which publishes the documented record and the research at JennifersLaw.org.
