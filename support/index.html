Fund the Campaign for Judicial Accountability

Gifts to Jennifer’s Law Action pay for the petitions, the public campaigns, and the legislative fight to end judicial immunity.

Major gifts

For a gift of $10,000 or more, a gift of appreciated stock, or a wire transfer, email Jennifer’s Law Action and Jennifer will respond personally. Wire and bank instructions are sent only by email, after your identity is confirmed, and are never posted on this site.

Gifts to Jennifer’s Law Action are not deductible as charitable contributions, and they are generally not subject to federal gift tax (26 U.S.C. § 2501(a)(6)). Please confirm the tax treatment of any gift with your own advisor.

Jennifer’s Law Action is legally separate from Jennifer’s Law Foundation and from Jennifer Canali personally. To support Jennifer’s own appeal, visit JennifersLaw.org.
